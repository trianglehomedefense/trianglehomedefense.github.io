Common signs around the home

What are you hearing or seeing?

Bats

Evening activity around vents or roof edges, attic sounds, staining, and concentrated droppings may indicate bat use.

Squirrels

Daytime scratching, gutter travel, chewed fascia, damaged soffits, and enlarged gaps may indicate squirrel access.

Mice & rodents

Droppings, odors, nesting material, chewing, and activity around crawlspace edges or utility penetrations may indicate access.

Snakes

Sightings near foundations, crawlspaces, garages, stored materials, or heavy cover may indicate favorable shelter or prey conditions.

Wildlife follows the structure

We go where the animals go.

Wildlife follows repeatable paths through rooflines, vents, soffits, attics, crawlspaces, foundations, and exterior gaps. We inspect the accessible areas most likely to explain the activity, document what we find, and build a practical plan for the property.

What we inspect

  • Rooflines, returns, soffits, and fascia
  • Attics and accessible interior voids
  • Crawlspaces, vents, and foundation edges
  • Exterior penetrations and construction gaps
  • Visible evidence and travel routes

A clear route forward

Inspect. Remove. Repair. Remediate. Prevent.

  1. 1. InspectIdentify evidence, travel routes, entry points, and contributing conditions.
  2. 2. RemoveAddress active wildlife appropriately when the work is within scope.
  3. 3. RepairCorrect observed access points with durable, animal-resistant materials.
  4. 4. RemediateAddress accessible debris, contamination, or damaged materials when included.
  5. 5. PreventRecommend corrections and maintenance that reduce recurring access.

Property-specific corrections

Repairs designed for the property.

When repair work falls within our scope, we develop a correction plan for the observed condition. The goal is to close the access point, restore the protective barrier, and match the existing construction as closely as practical using durable, animal-resistant materials.

Full cosmetic restoration, specialty fabrication, or replacement beyond the approved scope may be quoted separately or referred to another qualified contractor.
