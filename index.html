Quick answers before you call

Common wildlife questions in the Triangle

Can bats be excluded in North Carolina right now?

Yes. The statewide bat pup-rearing eviction moratorium runs May 1 through July 31. Beginning August 1, exclusion may resume when conditions and the approved scope allow.

I heard scratching but do not know what animal it is. Should I still call?

Yes. Time of day, where the sound is located, visible droppings, chewing and roof or crawlspace openings can help narrow the concern. You do not need to diagnose it first.

What do you look for after a snake sighting?

We look at visible access points, crawlspace and foundation conditions, stored materials, vegetation and signs of prey pressure around the structure.

Do you serve Raleigh, Fuquay-Varina, Apex and Clayton?

Yes. Those communities are within our Greater RDU service area. Priority coverage is south of Raleigh and along the US-1 corridor. Call or text and we will confirm availability before scheduling.
