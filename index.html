Good fit

The right call for the right kind of problem.

Triangle Home Defense handles wildlife access, inspections, exclusion, entry sealing, roofline, vent, crawlspace, and foundation access concerns, including bats, squirrels, birds, snakes, mice, and rodent prevention.

Common access concerns

Roofline, vent, crawlspace, foundation access, moisture and damage observations, recurring entry points, and visible wildlife pressure.

Calls we do not take

Triangle Home Defense does not handle domestic animal calls, lost pets, dogs, cats, livestock, animal cruelty investigations, law-enforcement matters, or routine spray-only pest programs.

Better resources

For injured or orphaned wildlife, bites, public-safety concerns, or animals in legal custody, contact the appropriate local agency, veterinarian, wildlife rehabilitator, or animal control.

Stewardship, not a one-off service call

A home is part shelter, part terrain.

Wildlife issues are often structural literacy problems. THD helps homeowners understand the edges where roof, water, heat, materials, and habitat meet.

Why the same opening keeps coming back

Animals follow repeatable routes. If the repair ignores the route logic, the house stays readable as shelter.

Why roofs and crawlspaces matter together

Access points can connect across the whole envelope. A roof return, vent, or crawlspace gap may be part of one larger system.

Why local terrain changes the inspection

Wooded edges, sheds, moisture, nearby food sources, and seasonal weather all shape how wildlife tests a home.

Field notes

Dig deeper without getting dumped on.

Edges are where problems start.

Wildlife reads transitions: siding to trim, roof to soffit, deck to crawlspace, brush to foundation. THD reads those transitions too.

Rooflines carry quiet traffic.

Returns, vents, channels, and overhangs create protected paths. Sealing works best when the route is understood.

The land changes the inspection.

North Carolina homes sit against tree lines, clay, stone, water, and heat. The inspection follows those pressures before recommending work.
